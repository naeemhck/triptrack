TripTrack Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 12, 2026

TripTrack Core Privacy Promise: Your location is shared strictly within authorized, active trip groups. Location tracking turns off automatically when a trip ends or when you toggle sharing off. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. Information We Collect

TripTrack collects the minimum necessary data to provide realtime group location sharing and itinerary tracking:

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, optional profile avatar, and Supabase user ID.
  • Precise Location Data: GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, accuracy, timestamp) collected while location sharing is enabled.
  • Background Location Data: Collected when you explicitly select "Allow Always" location access for active trips.
  • Route Leader History: When you are explicitly designated as an active trip's Route Leader, filtered GPS samples are retained as the group's canonical traveled route. Other members' samples remain latest-location records and are not converted into full location history.
  • Trip Stops & Notes: Manually marked stop titles, notes, interactive map pin coordinates, and automatically detected dwell stops.
  • Stop Photos: User-selected photos attached to trip stops, normalized to JPEG format (max 10 MB).
  • Expo Push Tokens: Device registration tokens used through Expo Push Service to deliver stop and stale-location notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Displaying live positions of group members on interactive trip maps.
  • Automatic dwell detection for stopping events during active road trips.
  • Sending push notifications to trip members when a stop is created or location becomes stale.
  • Providing chronological trip timelines and historical itineraries for authorized trip members.
  • Calculating on-route group separation and threshold alerts from the Route Leader's actual traveled path.

3. Location Sharing & Privacy Controls

Location sharing is strictly opt-in and trip-scoped. You can adjust your location sharing mode at any time in the app:

  • 🟢 Always Allow (Background & Foreground): Shares live location while app is open, in background, or when screen is locked.
  • 🟡 App Open Only (Foreground): Shares live location only while TripTrack is actively open on your screen.
  • ⚪ Sharing Disabled: Completely stops location updates and background tracking.

4. Data Retention & Security

Your application data is stored in Supabase PostgreSQL and private Supabase Storage with row-level security and storage access policies:

  • Only the latest shared location per ordinary member and trip is retained; it is overwritten by newer samples and deleted when the trip is completed or the account is deleted.
  • Filtered Route Leader geometry is retained with completed trip history for authorized trip members. It records the canonical path traveled, not every raw GPS callback.
  • Completed trip history and photos are accessible only to authorized current members of that trip.
  • Photo access requires authenticated trip membership. Uploads additionally require an active trip and ownership of the associated stop.

5. In-App Account Deletion & Rights

You have full control over your data. You may delete your TripTrack account at any time directly in the app under Settings/Profile or via our online request portal:

  • Account deletion immediately stops all active location tracking and background services.
  • Deletes your Expo push tokens, current location records, eligible memberships, and Supabase Authentication account.
  • Anonymizes the retained profile identity where historical trip ownership must remain intact.

6. Third-Party Data Processing

TripTrack uses trusted cloud infrastructure providers strictly to deliver service functionality:

  • Supabase: Authentication, PostgreSQL database, private photo storage, realtime updates, and server functions.
  • Expo Push Service: Push notification delivery through the device platform's FCM or APNs infrastructure.
  • OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap contributors: Interactive map styles, tiles, and map data.

7. Contact Us
