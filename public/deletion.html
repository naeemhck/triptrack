TripTrack Account & Data Deletion Request

In compliance with Google Play and Apple App Store user privacy policies, TripTrack provides full self-service account deletion in-app and web-based data deletion requests for all registered travelers.

Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the TripTrack app on your iOS or Android device.
  2. Navigate to Trip List / Profile Settings screen.
  3. Tap the red "Delete Account" button at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Confirm prompt. Your account, background tracking services, FCM push token, and local data will be deleted immediately.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

If you no longer have access to the TripTrack app on your device, submit a web deletion request below. Deletion requests are processed within 24 hours.

What Data Is Deleted?

  • Firebase Auth Account: Permanently deleted.
  • User Profile: users/{uid} document marked deleted and anonymized.
  • Push Notification Tokens: FCM tokens deleted from Firestore database.
  • Background Location Watcher: Tracking task and local async state cleared.
  • Trip Membership: UID removed from active member lists.
